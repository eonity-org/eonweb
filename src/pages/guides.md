These guides are for the people who **use** TYDAL and TYKERF: members who browse, upload and share resources, the administrators who look after an organization, and the people who build web pages with TYKERF. They are maintained alongside the code in each product's repository, so they always match the latest release.

To install, configure or build on TYDAL, see the [developer documentation](/developers/). For TYKERF, see its [developer guide](https://github.com/eonity-org/tykerf/blob/main/docs/developer-guide/README.md).

## TYDAL

TYDAL is where your organization's resources live: the library, the upload wizard with AI suggestions, tags, workspaces and the Vaults that share content outside TYDAL. ## FullFrame, an example client

[FullFrame](https://github.com/eonity-org/fullframe) is an independent application built on TYDAL that turns photographs into online exhibitions. Its user guides, for visitors, curators, invited authors, jurors and administrators, are in [its own repository](https://github.com/eonity-org/fullframe/blob/main/docs/user-guide/en/README.md). For how an external client like it uses TYDAL, see [FullFrame: an example client of TYDAL](/developers/fullframe/).
