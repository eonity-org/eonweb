TYDAL is the typed Digital Asset Layer: schemas structure your resources, and Vaults expose selected content to people, applications and AI agents. These guides introduce the main paths into the platform and link to the maintained documentation in the [public TYDAL repository](https://github.com/eonity-org/tydal).

## Choose your starting point

- **Run TYDAL:** start with [Getting started](/developers/getting-started/) and the repository's deployment guide.
- **Connect your AI:** use [API and integrations](/developers/api-and-integrations/) to choose between organization management and access to one Vault.
- **Build an application:** explore the HTTP API, TypeScript SDK and Vault interfaces in [API and integrations](/developers/api-and-integrations/), then see [how FullFrame uses TYDAL](/developers/fullframe/).
- **Use TYDAL or FullFrame:** the [user guides](/guides/) walk through each screen by role, from browsing the library to curating an exhibition.
- **Understand the model:** read [About TYDAL](/about/) for resources, collections, workspaces and Vaults.

## Source and contribution

TYDAL is released under [Apache-2.0](https://github.com/eonity-org/tydal/blob/main/LICENSE). The [contribution guide](https://github.com/eonity-org/tydal/blob/main/docs/CONTRIBUTING.md) covers development standards and pull requests. The [documentation index](https://github.com/eonity-org/tydal/blob/main/docs/README.md) links to detailed specifications and operational guides.

[Screenshots and walkthroughs](/developers/screenshots/) shows the interface with real captures. Setup commands and configuration reference live in the product repository so they can evolve alongside the code.
