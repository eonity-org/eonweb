Structured publishing,
with AI that asks first.

Structured publishing has yet to benefit from AI. TYSET is a structured publishing editor that opens MIF documents and keeps their structure, layout and styles, so existing publications can come along. Support for DITA is planned.

Built for publishers and technical writers who review, correct and translate their documents with an AI that works on one section at a time.

What it is for ↓Follow the launch on X ↗

For publishers

Review, correct
and translate.

The work around a publication is mostly reading, fixing and translating. That is where an AI helps most, and where a person must have the final word.

01Review

Read with a second pair of eyes.

The AI goes through one section at a time and flags what to look at: inconsistencies, unclear passages, wording that departs from the rest of the document. You decide what stays.

02Correct

Fix it where it lives.

Corrections are made on the structured text, not on a flattened copy, so formats and flow are kept. Every change is shown before it is applied and can be undone.

03Translate

Translate the content, keep the layout.

The text of a section is translated while its structure stays in place, so the translation comes back in the same document, ready for the translator or reviewer to check.

See it work

One request,
the whole document.

Asked to increase the contrast of the figures, TYSET’s assistant goes through the chapter figure by figure. Then you compare the pages before and after, as they print.

Whole publications

From manuscript
to press.

TYSET handles books made of many files, the formats that shape them and the PDF that goes to print.
